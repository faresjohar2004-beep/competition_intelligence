Main Task

The analytics indicate that most visitors remain on the landing page and do not progress deeply into the product.

The landing page is currently the bottleneck.

The goal is to increase engagement by moving visitors into the AstraVita experience as quickly as possible.

Do not redesign the website.

Instead, improve the user journey.
