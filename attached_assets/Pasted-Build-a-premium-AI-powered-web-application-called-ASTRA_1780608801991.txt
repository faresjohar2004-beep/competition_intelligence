Build a premium AI-powered web application called ASTRA.

Tagline:
"Meet The Person You're Becoming."

ASTRA is not a habit tracker, to-do list, productivity app, or fitness tracker.

ASTRA is a Future Self Simulator.

The entire app revolves around one concept:

Every action a user takes changes a realistic AI-generated version of their future self.

The app should feel futuristic, emotional, addictive, and visually stunning.

Design inspiration:
- Apple
- Tesla
- Duolingo
- Notion
- Arc Browser

Use dark mode by default.

Create a premium glassmorphism interface with smooth animations, beautiful gradients, modern typography, and highly polished interactions.

---------------------------------------------------
CORE CONCEPT
---------------------------------------------------

When a user joins ASTRA, they answer:

- Name
- Age
- Gender
- Height
- Weight
- Fitness level
- Income range
- Career
- Main goals
- Biggest struggles
- Daily habits

Based on these answers, ASTRA creates:

1. Current Self
2. Future Self (1 year)
3. Future Self (5 years)

The Future Self should be represented through:

- AI-generated character profile
- Health score
- Wealth score
- Discipline score
- Confidence score
- Knowledge score
- Life trajectory score

---------------------------------------------------
THE FUTURE SELF ENGINE
---------------------------------------------------

Every decision changes the future.

Examples:

Workout completed:
+2% Health
+1% Confidence

Skipped workout:
-1% Health

Read 20 pages:
+1% Knowledge

Created content:
+2% Creator Score

Saved money:
+1% Wealth

Users immediately see their Future Self evolve.

The Future Self becomes more successful, healthier, stronger, and more confident as positive actions are completed.

---------------------------------------------------
THE TIMELINE SYSTEM
---------------------------------------------------

Show users three timelines:

Current Path

Improved Path

Potential Peak Path

Users can switch between timelines and see how different choices affect their future.

---------------------------------------------------
AI LIFE SIMULATION
---------------------------------------------------

Generate realistic predictions.

Examples:

"If you continue this habit pattern, your fitness score may increase by 18% within 12 months."

"If your current savings rate continues, your projected wealth score increases by 22%."

The AI should explain WHY.

---------------------------------------------------
DAILY ACTIONS
---------------------------------------------------

Users log:

- Workouts
- Reading
- Learning
- Content creation
- Meditation
- Saving money
- Deep work
- Sleep
- Water intake

Every action affects future projections.

---------------------------------------------------

Create a visual life map.

Sections:

- Health

Display beautiful animated progress systems.

---------------------------------------------------
THE FUTURE LETTER
---------------------------------------------------

One of the main features.

The AI writes messages from the user's future self.

Examples:

"Thank you for not quitting."

"Your consistency changed everything."

"You almost gave up in Chapter 3. I'm grateful you kept going."

These messages should feel emotional and motivating.

Example:

"Today Faris chose growth and completed his workout despite low motivation."

At the end of the year users have an AI-generated story of their life.
