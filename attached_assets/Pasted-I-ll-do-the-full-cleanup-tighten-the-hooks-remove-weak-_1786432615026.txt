The original batch had a few problems: **too much “Stay in Sync” repetition, generic AI-style hooks like “unlock” and “harness the power,” unnecessary product mentions, and a few claims that were too absolute.**

The biggest fixes were:

**#6:** “30–60 minutes” was presented as the carb timing rule. That's appropriate for certain lighter/easier-to-digest foods, but larger meals generally need more time.

**#7:** “Skipping mobility increases injury risk” was too strong.

**#9:** The 20-second form method was weak enough that I replaced the entire topic.
